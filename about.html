ABOUT

MAIN BIO

I'm Elijah, a fullstack engineer who gets a kick out ofmessy systems and the moment they finally click.

Most of my work sits behind products people rely on daily — checkout flows, booking engines, dashboards that update in real time. I care about the boring parts: retries, idempotency, logs you can actually read. That's usually where "it just works" comes from.

I've shipped solo and inside teams, with clean specs and with requirements that changed mid-sprint. Both taught me the same lesson: make the system easy to change, because it will be.

These days I'm building at Morlabs — payments infrastructure and internal tooling, with a focus on making edge cases boring instead of scary.
Off the keyboard: long walks, strategy games, and poking at why some outages make the news and most don't.
THAT'S ME
HOW I WORK

Understand before you build

I'd rather spend a day asking obvious questions than a month building the wrong thing. Most "hard" problems turn out to be unclear problems.

ask twice, build once.

Design for the bad day

Networks partition, providers rate-limit, deployments roll back at 5pm. I build for those days on purpose, so they're unremarkable when they come.

fail loudly, recover calmly.

Small pieces, clear seams

Services, modules, migrations — I like boundaries you can point at. When something breaks, the seam tells you where to look.

boring architecture, exciting product.

Leave it documented

The best compliment a codebase can get is a new teammate shipping on day one. Runbooks, ADRs, and honest commit messages are part of the feature.

write it like they'll need it at 3am.
